# Sloped Tree Bench — decision log

A running record of the design decisions behind `bench-site.html` and
`projects/build-day.html`, so we don't re-litigate them and the *next* project
can learn from them. Newest at the bottom.

## Concept
- **Two-seat symmetric A-frame**, built for a specific spot: a ~20° wooded slope
  with a **vertical living ~54″ tree** directly behind it. It replaces an old grey
  plank-on-tree bench that failed at the backrest attachment.
- **Separate reclined backrest**, *not* a leg. The backrest bolts to the back
  legs; it is not part of the A-frame. (This was corrected early after an initial
  version used the backrest as the back leg.)

## Key geometry decisions
- **Symmetric A legs, 12° splay.** Kept symmetric on request (reference looked
  slightly asymmetric). 24″ base.
- **Slope handling:** front legs lengthened (~10″) to reach the downhill ground;
  the actual front-leg lean becomes **8.4°** (not 12°) because of the lowered foot.
  So: **back legs 12°, front legs 8.4°.**
- **5° whole-bench tilt, rear-down** ("you fall into the backrest"). Net installed
  recline ≈ 25° off vertical (20° backrest + 5° tilt); seat-to-back ≈ 110–120°.
  Flagged as a *perch* (seat ~22–27″ above the downhill ground) — **seat height &
  recline to be tuned on site at dry-fit.**
- **Tree tails:** the two seat rails extend ~40″ back as "tails" lagged to the
  trunk. Tree anchor approach (rigid lags vs flexible strap) = **decide on site.**
- **Tilt, don't bevel:** the 5°/20°/8.4° angles are achieved by tilting square-cut
  boards at assembly (more accurate with hand tools); only the legs + a few fronts
  carry an actual saw angle.
- **Center sag support:** a 57″ flat-2×4 seat span would sag (0.49″ vs 0.05″), so
  added a **center front leg AC** (in the front-leg plane, 1.5″ behind beam J) +
  **center seat rail EC** (angled 8.4° front on AC, square rear on the center post).
- **Front beam J = 57″, canted 8.4°** to laminate flat on AC; bottom rails get
  matching 8.4° angled fronts. All seat on one 8.4° plane.
- **Slats:** seat & back slats both full-width 57″ (unified look); one consistent
  **⅜″ gap** for both.

## Materials & fasteners
- **10 × 10-ft 2×4 + 1 × 8-ft 2×6 pine** (packs into 10 boards at ~7% waste).
- **No fold:** a folding backrest was explored (locking hinge) then dropped —
  **bolted, non-folding** (4 carriage bolts, post→rail→leg). Folding is a possible
  follow-up.
- **Pocket screws are all 2½″** (every board is 1½″ stock; 1½″ screws are for ¾″
  material). ~57 pocket + ~8 straight back-slat screws.
- **Slats are screw-only, NOT glued** (must float + stay replaceable); glue the
  frame joints only.
- Bolts spread **6″**, **7″** long, **fender washers + nyloc** (per structural
  review). All tree/bolt hardware **stainless or hot-dip galvanized**.

## Review outcomes (5-expert pass)
- **Weakest link = the tree-tail lag joint** (withdrawal into live wood). Mitigate
  or demote the tree to a flexible lateral-only tie.
- **All 5 feet** (incl. center leg) on stone/paver/standoff; moisture break + sealed
  end grain. Realistic life **10–15 yrs** with annual re-seal.
- Build aids: 8.4°/12° angle blocks, wedge cradles for the canted beams, pre-drill
  all pockets in the shop, fit EC/feet/tails on site.

## Site prep
- Demolish old bench; **don't level the whole footprint** (keep the slope). Level
  the **back pair** of footings to one height and the **front pair** to one (lower)
  height → matched-pair leg lengths + center leg fit on site.
