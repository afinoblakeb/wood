# <Project> — decision log

Newest at the bottom. Capture *why*, not just *what*, so choices don't get
re-litigated. See `projects/bench-decisions.md` for a worked example.

## Concept
- What it is, where it goes, what problem it solves.

## Key geometry decisions
- Overall size, critical angles, and the reasoning / constraints behind each.

## Materials & fasteners
- Lumber buy, fastener types + lengths, glue policy.

## Review outcomes
- Notable risks found and how they were resolved or deferred.

## Open / on-site decisions
- Things deliberately left to the dry-fit or build day.
