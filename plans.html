About these plans

Every plan on this site follows the same conventions so you always know what you're getting:

  • Actual dimensions. All measurements use the actual milled size of the lumber. A 2×4's real dimension is 1½" × 3½" — those are the numbers used throughout.
  • Complete cut list with quantities and lengths, plus a board-buying summary.
  • Full supply & hardware list so you can shop in one trip.
  • Dimensioned drawings (front, side, and exploded views).
  • Step-by-step assembly with a finishing guide.
  • Printer-friendly — use your browser's Print command for a clean shop copy.
